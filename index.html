Stefano Savastano, PhD

Physicist // Applied Scientist
Performer and Clown

My background education is Physics: I am a PhD graduate at Max Planck Insitute for Gravitational Physics & Humboldt University.

In recent years, I have expanded my personal research on human nature through the lens of artistic practices. I have trained and performed as physical Performer and Clown.

The common thread is the exploration of new, unknown spaces, off the map, where one can mirror oneself and question what exists.

I believe in the potential that arises from the meeting of scientific knowledge and artistic attitude, drawing from the universality of human and cosmic phenomena to unveil their hidden poetry.

That's why I enjoy to follow a trans-disciplinary approach and mix these languagese in my work, research and communication. :)
